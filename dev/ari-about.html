About Ari · Zorex AI

A thoughtful AI workspace for ideas, questions and ongoing work.

Ari is an AI assistant created by Zorex AI. It brings conversation and personal work organization into one place, helping people explore questions, develop ideas and keep related work together.

What Ari can do today

Conversations

Ask questions, get explanations, brainstorm, write and work through ideas in a conversational workspace.

Projects

Group related conversations into projects, return to ongoing work and keep each project organized.

Personal Library

Upload and keep images, documents and audio files in an account-specific Library, with file previews where supported and options to download or delete saved files.

Supported image understanding

Share supported images in eligible chat modes and ask Ari questions about what is visible.

Memory review

Add, edit, keep or remove details in a personal memory area. People remain in control of what is saved.

Copyable answers and code

Copy complete answers or individual code examples for use in notes, editors and other work.

A clear note about files

Ari can currently store documents and audio in the Library, but reading document contents and understanding or transcribing audio are not yet available. Uploading a file does not mean Ari has analysed it.

Built with curiosity and persistence

Ari is an independently developed project with roots in Nigeria. Behind the work is a Nigerian developer, a laptop and years of accumulated learning, experimentation and problem-solving. The aim is to turn that knowledge and persistence into practical AI tools that people can use in everyday work.

Ari continues to evolve. This page describes features that are available in the current workspace and will be updated as new capabilities become usable.

Your workspace

People sign in with a verified Google account. Conversations, projects, Library files and saved memories are scoped to the signed-in account. Use the controls in Ari to review and manage your own saved information.
